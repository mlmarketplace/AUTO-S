Terraform Policy Guidelines (Enterprise Environment)

General Principles:
- All infrastructure must comply with organizational security, cost, and compliance policies
- Policies are enforced during Terraform plan and apply phases
- Violations must be resolved before deployment

---

[SECTION: RESOURCE NAMING]

Policy:
- Resource names must follow naming conventions: <env>-<service>-<resource>

Violation:
- Non-compliant naming patterns

Recommended Action:
- Rename resources to match standard naming conventions

---

[SECTION: ENVIRONMENT RESTRICTIONS]

Policy:
- Production resources must not be modified without approval
- Destructive actions (delete, replace) are restricted in production

Violation:
- Attempt to delete or modify production resource

Recommended Action:
- Escalate for approval or use change management process

Risk Level: HIGH

---

[SECTION: SECURITY GROUP RULES]

Policy:
- No open access (0.0.0.0/0) on sensitive ports (22, 3389)

Violation:
- Security group allows unrestricted inbound access

Recommended Action:
- Restrict access to specific IP ranges

Risk Level: HIGH

---

[SECTION: TAGGING POLICY]

Policy:
- All resources must include mandatory tags:
  - environment
  - owner
  - cost_center

Violation:
- Missing required tags

Recommended Action:
- Add required tags before deployment

Risk Level: MEDIUM

---

[SECTION: INSTANCE TYPES]

Policy:
- Only approved instance types allowed in production

Violation:
- Use of unapproved or high-cost instance types

Recommended Action:
- Replace with approved instance types

Risk Level: MEDIUM

---

[SECTION: ENCRYPTION]

Policy:
- All storage resources must have encryption enabled

Violation:
- Unencrypted storage (EBS, S3, etc.)

Recommended Action:
- Enable encryption settings

Risk Level: HIGH

---

[SECTION: COST CONTROLS]

Policy:
- Resources must not exceed defined cost thresholds

Violation:
- Over-provisioned resources or unused services

Recommended Action:
- Optimize resource allocation

Risk Level: MEDIUM

---

[SECTION: STATE MANAGEMENT]

Policy:
- Terraform state must not be manually modified
- State locking must be respected

Violation:
- Force unlock or manual state edits

Recommended Action:
- Investigate lock and resolve safely

Risk Level: HIGH

---

Important Notes:
- Policy violations should not be bypassed
- All production changes require approval workflows
- If policy intent is unclear, escalate instead of guessing
