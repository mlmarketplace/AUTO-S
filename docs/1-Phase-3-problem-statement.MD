## Document the exact problem to be solved

**The Problem Statement**

Enterprises using Terraform Enterprise lack a reliable, unified, and safe system to diagnose, reason about, and resolve 
infrastructure issues across environments in real time.

This results in:

1) Slow incident resolution due to manual log analysis and fragmented tooling
2) Inconsistent decision-making driven by individual experience rather than systemized logic
3) Limited visibility into infrastructure state, especially across multiple accounts and environments
4) Delayed detection of policy violations and configuration risks
5) Uncontrolled cloud cost growth due to lack of proactive optimization
6) Overloaded support workflows handling repetitive, low-value queries

**The Critical information is scattered across:**

- Terraform runs and logs
- State files
- Policy engines like HashiCorp Sentinel
- Cloud provider APIs
