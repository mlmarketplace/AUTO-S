## Identify the primary user persona and daily workflow

Modern enterprises depend on complex, distributed infrastructure to run critical services. 
Managing it has become increasingly error-prone and difficult to scale.

#### Primary Persona

Developers, DEVOPS engineers and other members across the Line of businesses who are using the enterprise TFE 
for critical business solution's deployment.

#### Secondary Persona

1) SRE / Incident Responder
- Handles outages and critical failures
- Needs fast root cause + safe actions
2) Cloud FinOps / Cost Analyst
- Tracks infra cost
- Wants optimization suggestions with impact
3) Security / Compliance Engineer
- Ensures policies are enforced
- Cares about audit logs and traceability
